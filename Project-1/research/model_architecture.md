# Integrated model build specification

One pipeline: source history → reported/normalized reconciliation → personalized operating drivers → five-year IS/BS/CF → fail-loud checks → FCFF → WACC DCF → EV/equity/share bridge → cases/peers/reverse DCF → conditional decision and visible outputs.

Visa revenue architecture: service revenue tied to lagged payment volume and effective yield; processing revenue to processed transactions/yield; international revenue to cross-border volume/mix; other/value-added services separately. Historical growth-rate shortcut is permitted only if defended as a reduced-form approximation. Incentives reduce revenue and require expense-to-cash asset/liability timing. Document all fixed balances. Restricted escrow/client collateral are excluded from discretionary liquidity. SBC must be treated consistently across costs, cash, dilution and payouts; the legacy cash-compensation substitution is a modeling assumption requiring reconciliation.

FCFF = EBIT − unlevered cash operating taxes + operating D&A − capex − change in operating NWC, with other operating reinvestment explicitly mapped. Under a flat tax regime, EBIT × (1−T) is a shorthand; loss-year taxes require a specified tax/NOL policy. Exclude cash, funded debt and financing flows from NWC. Decide which settlement and incentive balances are operating, with symmetric asset/liability treatment. Test FCFF = FCFE − net borrowing + after-tax interest under the same tax, nonoperating and reinvestment policies. Interest/dividends/buybacks/net borrowing cannot become operating FCFF.

WACC = E/(D+E) × (Rf + beta × ERP) + D/(D+E) × Kd × (1−T). Source dates/tenors, currency, beta methodology, marginal borrowing cost, tax shield and market-value capital weights. Explain lease/pension/preferred policy; never silently use book equity. A range is a judgment informed by evidence, not management guidance.

Terminal: define fiscal cash-flow dates and actual discount exponents at the chosen valuation date. An October 2026 date cannot discount all FY2026 cash flows as future flows. Choose defensible forecast years/stub and information cutoff; FY2026 year-end does not mean FY2026 audited filings are available. Sustainable terminal FCFF must match steady growth, margins, taxes and reinvestment/ROIC; do not grow an unusually favorable year blindly. Require finite WACC > g and a coherent terminal cash flow.

EV = PV(explicit signed FCFF) + PV(terminal). Equity = EV + eligible excess cash + separately valued nonoperating assets − debt/debt-like claims − preferred/NCI/other claims as applicable. Credit each item once; restricted cash and forecast accumulated cash are not automatic addbacks. Choose valuation-date diluted economic share equivalents, reconcile Visa conversions/preferences, and distinguish period-end counts from weighted-average EPS shares. Keep cash/debt/share dates compatible. Avoid double counting convertible claims in deductions and denominator.

Outputs: statements.csv; articulation.csv; normalized_history.csv; fcff_bridge.csv; wacc.csv; enterprise_equity_bridge.csv; scenarios.csv; wacc_growth_grid.csv; reverse_dcf.json; peer_multiples.csv; summary.md; run_manifest.json. Every material input needs units, classification, period, source/locator, as-of and retrieval dates.

Tests: independent filed base-year benchmarks; each-year BS/cash/roll-forwards; incentive expense-to-cash; signed-flow retention; finite/rate/terminal boundaries; expected monotonicity holding operating flows and bridge fixed (positive-flow conditions stated); share/debt/cash bridge deltas; consistent peer definitions; scenario isolation; deliberately broken cash/disconnected FCFF/stale-share basis. Refuse results on failed gates. Cold run only after full integration.
