**Organic growth:** growth excluding acquisitions/divestitures, often also excluding FX according to a company’s definition. Same-store sales is a retailer-specific comparable-location measure. Visa’s processed transactions and constant-dollar payments/cross-border volumes help assess underlying activity, but they are not interchangeable with organic revenue growth: pricing, mix and incentives also matter. The ABG exercise uses 1.8% rather than 4.7% to isolate the supplied same-store growth assumption instead of carrying acquisition-driven reported growth into the existing store base.
