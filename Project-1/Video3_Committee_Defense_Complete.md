## Committee Pushbacks & Analytical Defense

### Network moat vs. valuation

**“Doesn’t Visa’s moat justify a premium?”**

Network scale, broad acceptance, and digital-payment growth support the business thesis. The forecast already includes substantial growth: annual net revenue rises from **$51.9B to $73.2B** over five years.

**Defense:** A premium still needs cash-flow support. The favorable case reaches about **$375/share**, below the reference price.

### Terminal value dependence

**“How reliable is a value driven mainly by distant cash flows?”**

**76.1% of enterprise value comes from the terminal period.**

- Base assumptions: **8.74% WACC · 3.0% terminal growth · 30% incremental terminal ROIC**.
- Growth requires reinvestment; the base terminal reinvestment rate is **10%**.
- Increasing terminal growth to **3.5%** raises value to **$284.26/share** after reinvestment.

**Defense:** The recommendation survives this test. The base remains a conditional estimate; one sensitivity cannot resolve every uncertainty.

### Consumer and cross-border downside

**“What if activity or fee income weakens?”**

- **Slower consumer spending:** Lower payment volume and transaction growth reduce revenue.
- **Weaker cross-border activity or fee yield:** Lower international revenue reduces profit and cash generation.
- **Higher client incentives:** Greater payments to clients reduce net revenue and cash flow.

**Defense:** Linked operating and valuation stresses produce approximately **$179/share** in the low case and **$129/share** in the combined downside. These broad stresses do not isolate cross-border fee risk alone.

### Peer comparison

**“Mastercard multiples suggest a higher value. Why the gap?”**

The saved Mastercard comparison indicates approximately **$381–$400/share**, closer to the reference price.

**Defense:** Peer multiples inherit another company’s market expectations. The DCF explicitly forecasts cash flows, fades growth, and funds terminal reinvestment. Investigate the disagreement; one peer does not justify mechanically averaging the results.

## Committee Decision & Reconsideration

### Decline entry; maintain a monitored watchlist

Reconsider when a lower price or stronger operating evidence supports a revised valuation.

- **Price discipline:** The saved model’s conditional entry level near **$227** requires refreshed filings and operating confirmation; it is not an automatic buy trigger.
- **Operating evidence:** Monitor transaction and cross-border growth, client incentives, and margins.
- **Risk changes:** Reassess after material interest-rate moves or legal developments.
- **Before entry:** Refresh cash, debt, diluted economic shares, and valuation assumptions.

### Analytical limits

October opening balances are estimated from June filings, and shares are dated proxies. Discount rates, long-run margins, and terminal returns involve judgment. The peer comparison relies on one company.

**Accounting checks support consistency. They do not prove forecast economics.**
